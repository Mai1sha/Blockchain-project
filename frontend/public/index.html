Learn About Our Project

Why This Project?

The Problem

Traditional professional-service platforms maintain provider verification, engagement histories, and payment records through centralized infrastructure. Clients and service providers depend on the platform to confirm important actions and resolve conflicting claims.

Our project explores using blockchain to maintain a shared, tamper-evident record of selected engagement actions while keeping sensitive information, private communication, and professional work off-chain.

Our Solution

Proposed Features

Our platform combines conventional web functionality with blockchain-based verification, engagement records, and cryptocurrency escrow.

Verified Service Providers

Service providers complete off-chain KYC verification and connect their blockchain wallets. Verification status can be recorded on-chain without exposing personal documents.

Engagement Tracking

Important engagement actions, including acceptance, delivery attestation, and completion, are recorded on the blockchain to create a tamper-evident history.

Smart Contract Escrow

Clients fund smart-contract escrow using cryptocurrency. After the client accepts the completed work, the provider can withdraw the payment.

Private Communication

Client-provider messages, project requirements, personal information, and professional deliverables remain off-chain to protect privacy.

How It Works

The proposed workflow follows a service engagement from provider verification to final payment.

01

Provider Verification

A service provider completes off-chain identity verification and links a blockchain wallet.

02

Create an Engagement

A client selects a provider, agrees on the service, and funds cryptocurrency escrow.

03

Submit the Work

The provider delivers the work privately and records a delivery attestation on-chain.

04

Complete and Pay

The client accepts the work, the engagement is marked completed, and the provider can withdraw the escrowed funds.

On-Chain vs. Off-Chain

On-Chain

  • Wallet-linked verification status
  • Engagement state transitions
  • Delivery and completion attestations
  • Cryptocurrency escrow transactions

Off-Chain

  • KYC documents and personal information
  • Service provider profiles and listings
  • Client-provider messages
  • Project files and deliverables

Blockchain records provide evidence of wallet actions, but do not independently prove real-world identity or the quality of completed work.
