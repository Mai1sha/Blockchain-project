# 0001 — Example architectural decision record

Delete this file once your team has a real decision to record, or rename it `0002-...` and keep it as a template reference.

## Status

Proposed / Accepted / Superseded

## Context

What forces (technical, team, deadline) make a decision necessary here?

## Decision

What did the team decide to do?

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result? What did the team give up?
